Tahini Miso Chicken

Seven ingredients, one bowl, a grill and rice. The marinade doubles as the sauce, so there is nothing else to make: mix it at breakfast and dinner is half an hour when you get in.

Prep10 min
Cook20 min
Marinade1 h+
Serves4
SourceTommy Cole
StatusTrial

Ingredients

Method

  1. Whisk the tahini, honey, miso, soy sauce, lime zest and water together until smooth.
  2. Mix in the diced chicken and leave in the fridge to marinate for at least 1 hour (much longer is fine).
  3. Lift the chicken out of the marinade and tip the remaining marinade into a saucepan.
  4. Bring the reserved marinade to a simmer and cook for 2 minutes, stirring continuously so it doesn't catch on the bottom of the pan.
  5. Cook the chicken under a grill for 10-15 minutes, until fully cooked through.
  6. Toss the cooked chicken through the sauce and serve on a bed of rice.
Trial notes: use low-salt soy and taste the sauce before adding anything else; if it's too intense, loosen it with a splash of water rather than more honey. No chilli and a mild savoury flavour, so plausibly kid friendly, but untested. Treat it as a mix-at-breakfast recipe: the hour of marinade is the only thing standing between this and a genuine quick-night dinner.

Source: Tommy Cole, The Meals I Cook for Premier League Footballers
