Bibimbap

Korean rice bowl with chicken bulgogi, seasoned vegetables, apple slaw, gochujang sauce and a runny fried egg. Five components and an overnight marinade: a proper weekend build, worth it once in a while.

Prep60 min
Cook30 min
Marinadeovernight
Serves4
SourceTommy Cole
StatusTrial

Ingredients

  • Bulgogi (start the day before)
  • Vegetables
  • Marinated cucumber
  • Gochujang sauce
  • Apple slaw
  • To serve

Method

  1. The day before: slice the chicken thin, sprinkle over the bicarbonate of soda and chill for 30 minutes, then rinse the bicarb off thoroughly with cold water.
  2. Mix the other bulgogi ingredients (soy, oyster sauce, sugar, mirin, plum syrup, sesame oil, grated apple), add the chicken and coat well. Rest in the fridge overnight.
  3. On the day: strain off and reserve the marinade, pat the chicken dry, then fry in a hot pan with neutral oil until fully cooked.
  4. Add a cornflour slurry to the reserved marinade and bring to the boil to thicken into a sauce. Stir it through the cooked chicken.
  5. Fry the carrots in a little oil with a pinch of salt for 2 minutes until slightly soft, then add half the grated garlic and fry for another minute.
  6. Fry the spinach in a little oil with a pinch of salt for 1 minute, then add the remaining garlic and fry for a further 30 seconds.
  7. Mix the cucumber marinade ingredients and stir in the sliced cucumber. Leave to marinate for 1 hour.
  8. Mix all the slaw ingredients together.
  9. Whisk the gochujang, honey, rice vinegar and lime juice and zest until well incorporated.
  10. Fry the eggs in a little sesame oil, keeping the yolks runny.
  11. Serve on a bed of rice dressed with the gochujang sauce, topped with the bulgogi, the vegetables, the slaw and a fried egg. Finish each bowl with a scatter of toasted sesame seeds and a drizzle of toasted sesame oil.
Trial notes: quantities are scaled up from a single-athlete portion and the source's vegetable amounts come out absurd (800 g spinach, 2 whole cabbages); the list above already halves the spinach, cabbage and apples, so correct after the first cook. The sesame finish is a house addition: the source served the bowl without it, and it's the defining bibimbap aroma. Optional crispy-bottom move: press each portion of dressed rice into a hot oiled pan for 2-3 minutes before topping, for the effect a stone bowl would give. The bicarb step is proper velveting: don't skip it with breast, and don't overcook in step 3. All the heat is in the gochujang sauce, added at the table, so serve the kids rice, bulgogi, plain carrots and slaw with the sauce on the side.

Source: Tommy Cole, The Meals I Cook for Premier League Footballers
