Turkey Meatballs

Air-fried turkey meatballs with an Italian-sausage lean (parmesan, fennel), in a two-speed tomato sauce: quick on a weeknight, caramelised-onion braise at the weekend.

Weeknight~35 min
Weekend~75 min
Serves4
SourceGordon Ramsay, house v2

Ingredients

  • Meatballs
  • Sauce & pasta

Method

  1. Stir the bicarb into the water, then soak the panko in it for ~10 minutes until it forms a soft paste (panade). The bicarb keeps lean turkey juicy and browning well.
  2. Mix the mince with the panade, grated onion, parmesan, egg, garlic, Worcestershire, Knorr, fennel, chilli flakes and pepper. No added salt: the Knorr, parmesan and Worcestershire carry it. Chill ~10 minutes while the sauce starts.
  3. With wet hands, roll into 20 meatballs.
  4. Sauce, weeknight: soften the diced onion in the olive oil 5-6 minutes, garlic 1 minute, melt in the anchovy. Weekend: caramelise the onion 15-20 minutes on medium-low until golden and sticky first, then the garlic and anchovy.
  5. Add the purée for 2 minutes, then tomatoes, parmesan rind, oregano and balsamic. Simmer 10 minutes (weeknight) or ~30 (weekend).
  6. Meanwhile air-fry the meatballs at 200°C for ~9 minutes until browned: single layer, no turning, no oil.
  7. Marry balls and sauce: 5 minutes on a weeknight, or a gentle 25-30 minute braise at the weekend, loosening with a splash of pasta water if it thickens.
  8. Cook the linguine al dente; toss with a little sauce, top with balls, parmesan and basil.
