Rice Noodles with Chickpea Red Curry Sauce

Cracked coconut cream with red curry paste, chickpeas roughly crushed for texture, and fresh lime leaves. Note: contains fish sauce and most red curry pastes contain shrimp paste.

Prep10 min
Cook15 min
Serves2
SourceRosa's Thai Cafe
StatusTrial

Ingredients

Method

  1. Cook the vermicelli in simmering water for about 10 minutes until soft, then drain and set aside.
  2. Heat half the coconut milk in a wok for about 2 minutes, until the oil starts to separate.
  3. Add the curry paste and fry for 3 minutes, stirring, until fragrant. Pour in the remaining coconut milk with the lime leaves and cook for 2 minutes.
  4. Meanwhile, roughly crush the chickpeas and bash the galangal in a pestle and mortar. Squeeze the tamarind pulp in its water and strain, keeping the liquid.
  5. Stir the chickpeas, galangal, sugar, tamarind liquid and fish sauce into the sauce and simmer gently for 10 minutes. Taste and balance sweet, sour and salty.
  6. Divide the noodles between bowls, spoon over the sauce and finish with basil, chillies, cabbage, cucumber and bean sprouts.

Source: Rosa's Thai Cafe: The Cookbook by Saiphin Moore
