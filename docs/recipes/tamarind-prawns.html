Tamarind Prawns

Glossy tamarind-glazed king prawns with brown rice and pak choi. Sweet, sharp and very fast once the rice is done.

Prep10 min
Cook35 min (mostly rice)
Serves4 (halve for two)
SourceGordon Ramsay
StatusTrial

Ingredients

Method

  1. Put the rice and water in a pan, bring to the boil, cover and cook on the lowest heat for 30 minutes. Take off the heat and leave covered for 10 minutes. (Or cook it in the Ninja on pressure.)
  2. Warm the tamarind, fish sauce and sweetener together in a small pan until glossy; taste and adjust.
  3. Heat the oil in a large frying pan over a high heat and cook the shallot and garlic for 4 to 5 minutes until softened and golden at the edges.
  4. Boil the pak choi for 3 minutes and drain.
  5. Add the prawns to the frying pan for 2 minutes, then pour in the glaze and toss for 2 to 3 minutes until the prawns are pink, cooked through and well coated.
  6. Serve the prawns over the rice with the pak choi, scattered with coriander.
Trial notes: book nutrition per serving (made with coconut oil and agave): 434 kcal, 26 g protein, 64 g carbs, 7 g fat. The book version uses coconut oil; olive oil works just as well.

Source: Gordon Ramsay's Ultimate Fit Food, p151
