Pizza

House dough, upgraded: less yeast, a 24-48 hour cold ferment, and a 2-minute bake in the pizza oven at 370°C. Makes 4.

Prep20 min
Ferment24-48 h (fridge)
Cook2-2½ min each
Makes4 pizzas
SourceHouse recipe

Ingredients

  • Dough
  • Topping

Method

  1. Autolyse: mix the flour and all 260 ml warm water until just combined. Rest 20-30 minutes.
  2. Add the salt, instant yeast (straight in - it never needs blooming) and oil. Mix on speed 2 until combined.
  3. Knead on speed 4 for 5-7 minutes until smooth and elastic. Rest, covered, 20 minutes.
  4. Divide into 4 balls (~170 g) and place in a lightly oiled, covered container.
  5. Cold ferment in the fridge, 24-48 hours (48 is better - use 2 g yeast if going to 48). This is where the flavour and browning come from.
  6. On the day, take the dough out 2 hours before cooking.
  7. Preheat the pizza oven to maximum (370°C stone). Stretch, top, launch: 2-2½ minutes, one half-turn at the midpoint. No par-bake.
  8. Top lightly: ~75 g sauce, ~80 g grated mozzarella per pizza.
  9. Domestic oven fallback: max temperature, stone preheated 45 minutes; par-bake 3-5 minutes, top, then 7-10 minutes.
  10. Rest 2 minutes; finish with extra virgin olive oil and basil.
Upgraded 11/06/2026, retuned 02/08/2026 for a 370°C oven: hydration down to 65% (much easier to stretch and launch, no loss in the crumb at a 2-minute bake), yeast straight in (blooming is for active dry, not instant), oil kept - it aids browning at this temperature, and dry grated mozzarella instead of fresh (fresh floods the centre with water over 2 minutes). Same-day route in a pinch: 5 g yeast, 3-4 hour bulk prove, ball, 30-60 min bench rest. Freezer batch: double the dough and freeze the spare balls at the dividing step (oiled, individually bagged, up to 3 months); revive overnight in the fridge plus 2 hours out - a frozen ball, jar sauce and grated mozzarella is a complete ~20-minute dinner.
