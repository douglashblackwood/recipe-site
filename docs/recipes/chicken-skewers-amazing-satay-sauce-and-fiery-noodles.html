Chicken Skewers, Satay Sauce & Fiery Noodles

Based on Jamie Oliver's 30 Minute Meals: satay chicken skewers with honeyed cashew noodles, for two. 'est.' marks spoon-to-gram conversions that are estimates.

Prep20 min
Cook15 min
Serves2
SourceJamie Oliver

Ingredients

  • Satay sauce
  • Chicken
  • Honey cashews
  • Noodles and dressing
Note: Honey is 5 g in total, split between the chicken glaze and the cashews.

Method

  1. If you're using wooden skewers, soak 4 of them in cold water. Metal skewers are better if wooden ones won't fit the air fryer.
  2. Make the sauce. Blitz the peanut butter, soy, coriander, chilli, garlic, ginger, lime zest and juice in a food processor. Add water until it's a spoonable paste and season to taste. Put half aside for serving.
  3. Cut each breast into long strips and thread them onto 4 skewers. Lightly score both sides.
  4. Rub the rest of the sauce into the chicken, then add the olive oil and salt.
  5. Air fry at 200°C (392°F) for 12-14 minutes, turning halfway, until the middle reaches 74°C (165°F). Brush the honey on for the last 2-3 minutes. In the pizza oven, raise the skewers on a rack, cook them hot and fast, turn them often, and add the honey right at the end.
  6. Soak the noodles in boiling water for 6 minutes, then drain them and rinse under cold water.
  7. Toast the cashews in a dry pan until golden. Take the pan off the heat and toss them with the honey.
  8. Make the dressing. Blitz the onion, chilli and coriander stalks, then mix in the soy, lime juice, sesame oil, fish sauce and olive oil. Toss it through the noodles.
  9. Top the noodles with the cashews and coriander leaves. Serve the skewers on top, with the saved sauce on the side.

Source: Jamie Oliver, 30 Minute Meals
