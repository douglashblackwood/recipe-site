Miso Glazed Salmon with Kimchi Fried Rice

A three-ingredient miso glaze on grilled salmon over punchy kimchi fried rice. The marinade is a five-minute job that keeps for days, so the cook-day work is short.

Prep20 min
Cook20 min
Marinade6-8 h
Serves4
SourceTommy Cole
StatusTrial

Ingredients

  • Miso salmon
  • Kimchi fried rice

Method

  1. Add the mirin, sake and miso to a saucepan and mix thoroughly until smooth.
  2. Bring to the boil while stirring, then take off the heat and leave until completely cool.
  3. Pour the cooled marinade over the salmon and leave in the fridge for a minimum of 6 hours.
  4. Lift the salmon out of the marinade and wipe off any excess.
  5. Cook under a hot grill for 8-10 minutes, until the internal temperature reaches 45°C (50°C for medium, 55°C for well done). An air fryer at 200°C gives a similar result.
  6. For the rice: heat the oil in a wok or large frying pan over medium-high heat.
  7. Add the red onion and stir-fry for 30 seconds, then add the garlic and ginger and fry for another 20-30 seconds until fragrant.
  8. Add the carrot, celery, red pepper and the white half of the spring onions. Stir-fry for 2-3 minutes until just tender but still crisp.
  9. Add the cooked rice, breaking up any clumps. Stir-fry for 2 minutes until hot and well mixed through the vegetables.
  10. Stir in the soy sauce, fish sauce, gochujang, kimchi and honey. Toss thoroughly so the rice is evenly coated.
  11. Off the heat, add the lime zest and juice. Toss again and taste, adjusting with a little more soy or lime if needed.
  12. Serve the salmon on a bed of the rice, topped with the sliced green half of the spring onions.
Trial notes: all the heat lives in the rice, not the salmon, so hold back a portion of plain rice and vegetables before step 10 and the kids get the same meal without the gochujang and kimchi. Oil halved from the source's 4 tbsp; restore it if the rice catches. The equal-parts mirin-sake-miso glaze is the real takeaway: it works just as well on cod or chicken.

Source: Tommy Cole, The Meals I Cook for Premier League Footballers
