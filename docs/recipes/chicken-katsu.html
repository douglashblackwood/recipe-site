Chicken Katsu

Panko-crusted chicken with a smooth, glossy katsu curry sauce. The sauce makes a big batch and freezes beautifully: double it and the next cook is a 25-minute dinner.

Prep25 min
Cook35 min
Marinade4 h+
Serves4
SourceTommy Cole
StatusTrial

Ingredients

  • Chicken
  • Katsu sauce
  • To serve

Method

  1. Place each chicken breast between two pieces of baking paper and bash with a saucepan or rolling pin to tenderise and even out the thickness.
  2. Marinate the chicken in the soy sauce and mirin, in a bag or covered dish, for at least 4 hours.
  3. Pat the chicken dry and coat lightly in flour, then egg, then panko.
  4. Air fry until the internal temperature holds 65°C for 5 minutes (or deep fry at 175°C for the fullest texture). Season with a little salt.
  5. For the sauce: sweat the onion, carrots, garlic and ginger in a saucepan over medium heat for 8-10 minutes, until softened but not coloured.
  6. Add the curry powder and turmeric and cook for 1-2 minutes, stirring constantly, until fragrant.
  7. Pour in the chicken stock and reduce by about half.
  8. Add the coconut milk, bring to a gentle simmer and cook uncovered for 15-20 minutes, until the vegetables are very soft.
  9. Blend with a stick blender until completely smooth and glossy.
  10. Too thick: add a splash of stock or water. Too thin: return to the heat and reduce. Taste and adjust the salt. Slice the chicken and serve on rice with the sauce over.
Trial notes: the source says "reduce the stock by 3/4", which is ambiguous; reducing by half is the safe reading, since you can always cook it down further after the coconut milk goes in. Coconut milk, not cream, so it clears the house no-heavy-dairy rule. Batch the sauce, not the chicken: the sauce freezes well, breaded chicken doesn't. Counts as a curry against the fortnightly curry limit.

Source: Tommy Cole, The Meals I Cook for Premier League Footballers
